PIXEL BRAWL

A tiny two-player arcade fighting game — no installation required.

PLAYER 1 — BLUE
Move: A / D · Jump: W
Punch: F · Kick: G
PLAYER 2 — RED
Move: ← / → · Jump: ↑
Punch: K · Kick: L

Tip: get close to your opponent before punching or kicking. Works in a modern Windows browser; press a key once the game is focused.
